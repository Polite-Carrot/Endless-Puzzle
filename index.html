            Answering

            Every question has four answers and one is right. Tap the one you think it is — or press 1–4 on a keyboard. Green is right, red is wrong.

            The Climb

            100 levels, each a ladder of 20 rungs, one right answer at a time. Early levels are mostly easy; later ones get properly tough. Within a level the questions get harder as you climb.

            Safety nets sit at rungs 4, 8, 12 and 16. Get one wrong and you fall back to the last net you passed — and meet a different question on the way back up.

            Reach the top to unlock the next level. No falls earns ★★★, one or two falls ★★, more than that ★.

            Daily Quiz

            Five questions a day, from easy to extra hard. Everyone gets the same quiz, and you only get one go — so share your result and see how friends do.

            Play every day to build a streak. A new quiz arrives at midnight.

            🌍 Geography🪐 Space➗ Maths

            Quick Play

            Pick a difficulty, the categories you like and how many questions, then deal. No ladder and no falling — just see how many you get right.

            Every quiz has a seed. Give a friend the same seed and settings and they get exactly the same quiz.

            50/5025

            Coins & the 50/50

            Every right answer earns coins. Harder questions pay more:

            Easy 5
            Normal 10
            Hard 15
            Extra Hard 25

            Stuck? The 50/50 removes two wrong answers. It costs 25 coins, and the price doubles every time you use it in the same game. A question answered with a 50/50 pays half.

            Reset everything?

            Your coins, Climb levels and stars, daily streak and Quick Play records will all be wiped. This can’t be undone.
