          Answering

          Every question has four answers and one is right. Tap the one you think it is — or press 1–4 on a keyboard. Green is right, red is wrong.

          The Climb

          Climb 20 rungs, one right answer at a time. The questions get harder as you go: easy, normal, hard, then extra hard for the last four.

          Safety nets sit at rungs 4, 8, 12 and 16. Get one wrong and you fall back to the last net you passed — or to the bottom if you haven’t reached one yet.

          Daily Quiz

          Five questions a day, from easy to extra hard. Everyone gets the same quiz, and you only get one go — so share your result and see how friends do.

          Play every day to build a streak. A new quiz arrives at midnight.

          51020

          Quick Play

          Pick how many questions and how hard, then go. No ladder and no falling — just see how many you get right.

          Coins & the 50/50

          Every right answer earns coins. Harder questions pay more:

          Easy 5
          Normal 10
          Hard 15
          Extra Hard 25

          Stuck? The 50/50 removes two wrong answers. It costs 25 coins, and the price doubles every time you use it in the same game. A question answered with a 50/50 pays half.

          Reset everything?

          Your coins, best climb, daily streak and Quick Play records will all be wiped. This can’t be undone.
